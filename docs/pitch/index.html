The story · 1 of 3

The siren does not ask if you heard it.

An evacuation order hits the phones. Walter is 87, bedridden, alone. María answers in Spanish: no car, on oxygen. George is deaf — a call he cannot use. The alert already went out. Nobody knows who got out.

The story · 2 of 3

Responders knock on empty doors.

The people who already left still get welfare checks. The people who cannot leave wait. Apps do not reach landlines. Broadcast alerts never close the loop.

So we built the loop.

The story · 3 of 3

Headcount calls until everyone is counted.

A real phone call — or a text if they are deaf or hard of hearing. Speak freely, or press 1, 2, or 3. Missed? Try again. Three misses? A person goes to the door. Family gets a text the moment their person is safe.

Gemini understands. Plain, tested code decides.

Every person counted.
Every person home.

Built at Hack for Humanity — Laksh, Dat, Alex, and Dhruv.

Not an official alert system. In an emergency, call 911.
